SECTION 1: CITY, LIFE, WEATHER, AND ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CITY PROFILE & COST OF LIVING:
  * Gdańsk is a historic Baltic port city and part of the vibrant "Tricity" metropolis (along with Sopot and Gdynia).
  * Highly affordable compared to Western Europe, with student living costs estimated between €500–€700/month (including shared student housing, food, transport, and leisure).
  * Modern, efficient public transport system (trams, buses, and SKM commuter trains) seamlessly connecting the entire metropolitan coast.

- CLIMATE & WEATHER:
  * Temperate maritime climate influenced strongly by the Baltic Sea.
  * Summers are warm and breezy (averaging 18°C to 25°C), making the beaches incredibly popular.
  * Winters are cold, damp, and windy (averaging -3°C to 3°C), with occasional snowfall and frozen coastal landscapes.

- STUDENT LIFE & OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES:
  * Exceptional student lifestyle due to Tricity's unique layout: Gdańsk offers rich history and culture, Sopot acts as the main party and nightlife capital with famous beach clubs, and Gdynia offers modern coastal vibes.
  * Endless outdoor recreation: vast sandy beaches on the Baltic Sea, coastal cycling paths, windsurfing, sailing, and hiking in the nearby Tricity Landscape Park (Trójmiejski Park Krajobrazowy).

- CAREER & INDUSTRY OPPORTUNITIES:
  * A rapidly expanding tech hub in Poland with strong software, maritime engineering, and R&D sectors.
  * Local opportunities at multinational tech companies (such as Intel's largest European R&D center, Amazon, and Boeing) as well as numerous emerging Baltic tech startups.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDY PROGRAMS & ACADEMIC FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAIN ACADEMIC TRACKS:
  1. Applied Informatics (Stosowana Informatyka - BSc / MSc)
     * Focus: Scientific computing, computer graphics, physics-based simulations, software modeling, database systems, and practical programming languages.
     * Best for: Students who want to combine core software development with real-world physical simulations, computational graphics, or hardware integration.

  2. Applied Mathematics & Mathematical Modeling (Matematyka Stosowana - BSc / MSc)
     * Focus: Advanced numerical methods, statistical analysis, mathematical modeling in engineering, and machine learning algorithms.
     * Best for: Students looking for a highly analytical background, aiming for careers in data science, predictive modeling, or quantitative analysis.

  3. Nanotechnology & Computer Simulations (Nanotechnologia - MSc)
     * Focus: Computer-aided materials design, quantum and classical particle simulations, computational physics, and molecular modeling on supercomputers.
     * Best for: Students interested in computational science, high-performance computing (HPC), and simulating molecular or structural systems.

- INSTRUCTION & PRACTICAL ENGAGEMENT:
  * The faculty places a strong emphasis on computational laboratory work, exposing students to advanced simulation software, numerical engines, and scientific computing clusters.
  * A wide range of specialized courses is offered fully in English, tailored for international mobility and Erasmus+ students.
