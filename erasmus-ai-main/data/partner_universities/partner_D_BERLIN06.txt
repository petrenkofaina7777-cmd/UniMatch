SECTION 1: CITY, LIFE, WEATHER, AND ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CITY PROFILE & COST OF LIVING:
  * Berlin is a large, diverse capital with an intense cultural and student scene; housing is difficult, and a realistic budget is about €1,000–€1,400/month.
  * An extensive U-Bahn, S-Bahn, tram, and bus network makes the city easy to navigate; cycling is common, although distances can be large.

- CLIMATE & WEATHER:
  * Summers are mild to warm (about 20–28°C), while winters are cold and grey (roughly -1–6°C) with rain, wind, and occasional snow.

- STUDENT LIFE & OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES:
  * Berlin offers exceptional nightlife, museums, concerts, international food, political and cultural groups, university clubs, and year-round events.
  * Large parks, lakes, forests, canals, cycling routes, and nearby Brandenburg support swimming, running, hiking, and weekend escapes.

- CAREER & INDUSTRY OPPORTUNITIES:
  * Berlin is a leading European startup and technology hub, especially for fintech, e-commerce, software, AI, creative technology, consulting, and digital public services.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDY PROGRAMS & ACADEMIC FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAIN ACADEMIC TRACKS:
  1. Business Information Systems (BSc/MSc)
     * Focus: Software and databases alongside business administration, process design, enterprise systems, project management, and digital transformation.
     * Best for: Students who want to bridge IT and organizational or management problems.

  2. Business Intelligence and Process Management (MSc)
     * Focus: Big-data-supported decision making, analytics, business intelligence, process modelling, optimization, and organizational value creation.
     * Best for: Students interested in data-driven management, consulting, or enterprise technology.

  3. International Business and Digital Management (BSc/MSc subjects)
     * Focus: International management, economics, digital business models, operations, innovation, and selected information systems topics.
     * Best for: Students seeking a business-heavy exchange with technology and analytics components.

- INSTRUCTION & PRACTICAL ENGAGEMENT:
  * HWR Berlin is strongly applied, using case studies, projects, presentations, teamwork, and close links with employers and public institutions.
  * The Department offers an undergraduate General English Stream and fully English-taught Master's options; many regular modules are in German, so exchange students must use the current English course catalogue.
