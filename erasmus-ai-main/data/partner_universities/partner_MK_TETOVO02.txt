SECTION 1: CITY, LIFE, WEATHER, AND ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CITY PROFILE & COST OF LIVING:
  * Tetovo is a multicultural university city near the Šar Mountains, with a relaxed pace and very affordable living; students may budget about €400–€700/month.
  * The centre and campus surroundings are manageable on foot, with buses and taxis for longer trips; Skopje is reachable for major-city services and flights.

- CLIMATE & WEATHER:
  * Summers are hot and relatively dry (often 25–33°C); winters are cold (roughly -2–7°C), with rain in the valley and substantial snow in the mountains.

- STUDENT LIFE & OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES:
  * The university's multilingual community, clubs, cafés, restaurants, and events give the city a strongly student-oriented social life.
  * Popova Šapka and the Šar Mountains provide skiing, hiking, mountain biking, climbing, and scenic weekend trips.

- CAREER & INDUSTRY OPPORTUNITIES:
  * Opportunities include software outsourcing, telecommunications, digital services, entrepreneurship, and remote work, with a larger technology ecosystem in nearby Skopje.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDY PROGRAMS & ACADEMIC FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAIN ACADEMIC TRACKS:
  1. Computer Sciences (BSc/MSc)
     * Focus: Programming, algorithms, data structures, databases, operating systems, networks, and general software development.
     * Best for: Students seeking a balanced foundation across core computer science subjects.

  2. Software Engineering and Web/Mobile Systems (BSc/MSc modules)
     * Focus: Software lifecycle, web and mobile development, databases, cloud-oriented services, teamwork, and applied projects.
     * Best for: Students who prefer building applications and learning through practical development.

  3. Computer Engineering and Business Informatics (BSc)
     * Focus: Computer architecture, networks and systems, plus the use of IT to support organizations and business processes.
     * Best for: Students attracted to hardware-software integration or the intersection of technology and business.

- INSTRUCTION & PRACTICAL ENGAGEMENT:
  * Teaching is applied and includes labs, assignments, team projects, presentations, and links to regional employers.
  * English-language Computer Science programs are offered, alongside Albanian and Macedonian options; Erasmus students should confirm the live English course list for their semester.
