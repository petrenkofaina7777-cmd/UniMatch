SECTION 1: CITY, LIFE, WEATHER, AND ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CITY PROFILE & COST OF LIVING:
  * ESIEE Paris is east of central Paris in Noisy-le-Grand. The region offers unmatched cultural variety but high costs; a typical student budget is about €1,000–€1,500/month.
  * The RER A connects the campus area to central Paris; metro, trains, buses, cycling, and walking cover most daily travel.

- CLIMATE & WEATHER:
  * Summers are mild to warm (about 20–28°C) and winters cool (roughly 2–8°C), with rain spread across the year and occasional heatwaves or light snow.

- STUDENT LIFE & OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES:
  * Campus clubs combine with Parisian museums, concerts, nightlife, festivals, cafés, sports, and a very international student community.
  * Parks, the Marne river, Fontainebleau forest, and regional cycling routes provide outdoor options beyond the dense city.

- CAREER & INDUSTRY OPPORTUNITIES:
  * Greater Paris is France's largest employment market for software, AI, finance technology, cybersecurity, consulting, electronics, startups, and research.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDY PROGRAMS & ACADEMIC FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAIN ACADEMIC TRACKS:
  1. Computer Science, Algorithms and Development (Engineering)
     * Focus: Algorithms, programming, software architecture, databases, web systems, and development projects.
     * Best for: Students targeting software engineering and full-stack or backend development.

  2. Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (Engineering)
     * Focus: Statistics, machine learning, data processing, optimization, and AI applications.
     * Best for: Students with solid mathematics who want practical data and AI experience.

  3. Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity / Embedded Systems (Engineering/Master)
     * Focus: Secure intelligent systems, networks, cryptography, embedded platforms, electronics, and connected devices.
     * Best for: Students interested in security, IoT, intelligent hardware, or interdisciplinary engineering.

- INSTRUCTION & PRACTICAL ENGAGEMENT:
  * ESIEE emphasizes engineering projects, laboratories, teamwork, internships, and links with companies and applied research.
  * The AI and Cybersecurity international program is fully in English, and some semesters in Computer Science, Data Science/AI, and Embedded Systems are available in English; many other courses require French.
