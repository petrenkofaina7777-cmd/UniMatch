SECTION 1: CITY, LIFE, WEATHER, AND ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CITY PROFILE & COST OF LIVING:
  * Ilmenau is a small, affordable campus town in Thuringia with a close-knit student community; a common student budget is about €700–€950/month.
  * The compact town is easy to walk or cycle, local buses cover outlying areas, and trains connect to Erfurt and the wider region.

- CLIMATE & WEATHER:
  * Summers are mild (about 18–25°C); winters are cold (roughly -4–4°C) with frequent frost and more snow than many German lowland cities.

- STUDENT LIFE & OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES:
  * Campus clubs, student radio, cultural groups, sports, parties, and university events create an active community despite the town's small size.
  * The Thuringian Forest is immediately accessible for hiking, trail running, cycling, climbing, cross-country skiing, and winter sports.

- CAREER & INDUSTRY OPPORTUNITIES:
  * The university and region are strong in optics, sensor technology, communications, embedded systems, automation, scientific computing, and engineering research.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDY PROGRAMS & ACADEMIC FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAIN ACADEMIC TRACKS:
  1. Mathematics and Applied Mathematics (BSc/MSc)
     * Focus: Analysis, algebra, numerical methods, optimization, probability, modelling, and scientific computing.
     * Best for: Students who enjoy rigorous mathematics and computational problem-solving.

  2. Technical Physics and Data-Intensive Natural Science (BSc/MSc)
     * Focus: Experimental and theoretical physics, measurement, simulation, materials, optics, and computational analysis.
     * Best for: Students interested in scientific computing or technology grounded in physics.

  3. Interdisciplinary Computing, Signals and Modelling (MSc subjects)
     * Focus: Mathematical modelling, signal processing, data analysis, algorithms, optimization, and applications shared with engineering faculties.
     * Best for: Students who want computation linked closely to mathematics, communications, or engineering research.

- INSTRUCTION & PRACTICAL ENGAGEMENT:
  * Teaching is academically rigorous and combines lectures, exercises, laboratories, seminars, projects, and research-oriented work.
  * Many Bachelor's courses are in German, while selected Master's programs and exchange subjects are available in English; the precise English offering must be checked for each semester.
