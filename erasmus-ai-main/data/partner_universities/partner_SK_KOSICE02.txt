SECTION 1: CITY, LIFE, WEATHER, AND ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CITY PROFILE & COST OF LIVING:
  * Košice is the second-largest city in Slovakia, acting as the historical and economic hub of the eastern region.
  * Very affordable cost of living for students (estimated at €400–€600/month including shared dorms/apartments, food, and social life).
  * Extremely walkable, centered around a vibrant medieval main street (Hlavná ulica) filled with cafés, pubs, and historic architecture.

- CLIMATE & WEATHER:
  * Four distinct seasons.
  * Summers are warm and pleasant (averaging 20°C to 28°C), ideal for outdoor terraces.
  * Winters are cold, snowy, and freezing (averaging -5°C to 2°C), perfect for nearby skiing and Christmas markets.

- STUDENT LIFE & OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES:
  * Home to a massive student population across multiple universities, resulting in a good nightlife (numerous student clubs, underground bars, and concerts).
  * Highly active outdoor options: surrounded by nature, including the Jahodná recreation area, Bankov, and close proximity to the Slovak Paradise National Park (Slovenský raj) and the High Tatras for hiking, climbing, and winter sports.

- CAREER & INDUSTRY OPPORTUNITIES:
  * Known as the IT hub of Slovakia, powered by the "Košice IT Valley" association.
  * Strong presence of international tech corporations (T-Systems/Deutsche Telekom IT, Ness, FPT) and growing startup ecosystems, offering excellent local internship and networking prospects.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDY PROGRAMS & ACADEMIC FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAIN ACADEMIC TRACKS:
  1. Informatics (Informatika - BSc / MSc / PhD)
     * Focus: Strong theoretical foundations, logic, advanced data structures, cryptography, network security, and algorithm optimization.
     * Best for: Students aiming for deep computer science theory, backend engineering, or research paths.

  2. Applied Informatics (Aplikovaná informatika - BSc / MSc)
     * Focus: Practical software engineering, web technologies, database administration, mobile application development, and agile methodologies.
     * Best for: Pragmatic developers looking to build concrete applications and collaborate directly with IT companies.

  3. Data Analysis and Artificial Intelligence (Analýza dát a umelá inteligencia - BSc / MSc)
     * Focus: Machine learning, neural networks, statistical modeling, data mining, big data architectures, and predictive analytics.
     * Best for: Students who love mathematics, statistics, and training smart AI models.

  4. Interdisciplinary Informatics (Medziodborové štúdium - BSc)
     * Focus: Joint degrees combining Computer Science with another natural science (Physics, Biology, Chemistry, Geography, or Mathematics).
     * Best for: Students interested in specialized niches like Bioinformatics, GIS (Geographic Information Systems), or Computational Physics.

- INSTRUCTION & PRACTICAL ENGAGEMENT:
  * Heavy emphasis on hands-on project work, small seminar groups, and industry-sponsored thesis topics in collaboration with local IT companies.
