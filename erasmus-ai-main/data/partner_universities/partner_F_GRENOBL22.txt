SECTION 1: CITY, LIFE, WEATHER, AND ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CITY PROFILE & COST OF LIVING:
  * Grenoble is a compact Alpine university city with an active, international atmosphere; a realistic student budget is about €850–€1,200/month, depending mainly on housing.
  * The centre and campuses are bike-friendly and linked by trams and buses; many daily trips can be made by bicycle or on foot.

- CLIMATE & WEATHER:
  * Summers are warm (often 25–30°C), while winters are cold (roughly 0–8°C in the city) with rain and occasional snow; heavy snow is common in the surrounding mountains.

- STUDENT LIFE & OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES:
  * A large student population supports associations, cafés, concerts, bars, cultural events, and university sports.
  * The Alps make Grenoble exceptional for hiking, climbing, skiing, cycling, trail running, and weekend trips to nearby mountain parks.

- CAREER & INDUSTRY OPPORTUNITIES:
  * Grenoble is a major French deep-tech and research cluster, with strong activity in semiconductors, embedded systems, AI, scientific computing, and cybersecurity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDY PROGRAMS & ACADEMIC FOCUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAIN ACADEMIC TRACKS:
  1. Computer Science / MoSIG (MSc)
     * Focus: Software systems, cloud and distributed computing, data infrastructure, graphics, vision, robotics, and high-quality software components.
     * Best for: Students seeking advanced computer science with strong research links and demanding technical content.

  2. Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (MSc)
     * Focus: Machine learning, data engineering, optimization, statistics, computer vision, and interactive intelligent systems; mathematically intensive.
     * Best for: Students comfortable with mathematics who want AI, data, or research-oriented careers.

  3. Applied Mathematics, Optimization and Cybersecurity (MSc/Engineering)
     * Focus: Scientific computing, operations research, combinatorial optimization, cryptography, networks, systems, cloud, and security.
     * Best for: Students interested in algorithms, mathematical modelling, security, or high-performance technical work.

- INSTRUCTION & PRACTICAL ENGAGEMENT:
  * Teaching combines rigorous theory with laboratories, team projects, research exposure, and industry-connected internships.
  * Several international Master's tracks are fully taught in English; exchange students must check the current course catalogue because regular engineering courses may be in French.
