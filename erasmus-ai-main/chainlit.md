# UniMatch 🎓✈️

Tell me what you're looking for in an Erasmus exchange — academic focus, city vibe,
climate, cost of living, outdoor activities, whatever matters to you — and I'll match
you against our 26 partner university profiles.

**Example prompts:**
- "I want a warm city with good hiking nearby, and I want to focus on hands-on web development."
- "I love math and machine learning, cost of living matters a lot to me."

If your prompt is too vague I'll ask a quick follow-up before matching you.
